About MSP 790

A Bronx-rooted label and service platform built around artist growth, visual execution, and work that carries discipline behind it.

MSP 790 Payback Music was built to hold more than music. It is a space for artist identity, performance, visual storytelling, choreography, filming, digital presentation, and service work to reinforce each other instead of living in separate lanes.

Why It Exists

The label exists for people who want more than exposure. It is meant for creators who want standards, collaboration, and structure strong enough to support growth over time.

How It Works

MSP 790 connects artist development with practical services. Booking, visuals, websites, choreography, and client work all feed back into the larger movement.

Leadership

OG StarMoney

Founder, performer, and the main creative face of the platform. The role centers on identity, performance, choreography, and building a presence that feels complete on and off stage.

K-Money Music

CFO and production-minded execution support across filming, editing, websites, service delivery, and the systems that help the label operate with more polish.
