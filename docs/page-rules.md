# Page Rules

## Homepage

The homepage is:
- brand-first
- high-authority
- cinematic
- controlled

### Hero hierarchy
1. main name / identity
2. tagline
3. slogan

### Hero spacing
- tight-balanced
- compact, not cramped
- slightly above center vertically

### Hero visual treatment
- controlled premium glow (B+)
- sharp text
- clear contrast
- background must not compete with text

### Panels
- armored aero
- dimensional
- subtly edge-lit
- no red-filled boxes

### Navbar
- transparent at top
- medium-solid dark surface on scroll
- subtle depth
- no generic flat bar feel

## About page

About page should feel:
- real
- confident
- not speechy
- not corporate

It should explain:
- who the label is
- why it exists
- what it builds
- the foundation behind it

## Services page

Services should feel:
- structured
- serious
- not salesy
- not one-off
- built around long-term growth and consistency

## Artist pages

Artist pages must:
- preserve artist voice
- keep structure consistent
- avoid flattening individuality

The system is consistent.
The artists are not.

## K-Money page

K-Money page should feel:
- slightly deeper in atmosphere
- more technical
- more structured
- slightly richer in system feel
- not louder than the rest of the site
