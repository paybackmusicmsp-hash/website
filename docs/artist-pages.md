# Artist Page Rules

## Principle

The platform controls structure.
The artist controls voice.

## What to preserve

- artist-submitted tone
- personality
- imperfect but real phrasing where readable
- individuality

## What to refine

- readability
- spacing
- obvious grammar if it blocks clarity

## Do not

- rewrite artists into brand voice
- flatten everyone into the same tone
- make pages feel corporate

## K-Money-specific note

K-Money page may lean slightly more into:
- execution
- systems
- technical creative identity
- deeper atmosphere

But it must still belong to the same overall site.
