# Content Rules

## Voice

All public-facing copy must feel:
- direct
- grounded
- human
- intentional
- confident without hype

Do not write in a:
- corporate
- generic
- startup
- buzzword-heavy
- “AI polished” tone

## Text editing rule

If text is already good:
- do not change it

Only edit text if:
- clarity is broken
- grammar is clearly incorrect
- readability is poor
- older placeholder wording is still present and has approved replacement text

## Name rule

Always use:
- MSP 790 Payback Music

Do not abbreviate to:
- MSP
- MSP 790

Later references in the same section may use:
- the label
- the platform
- the movement

But do not casually shorten the official name.

## Protected slogan

Keep exactly:
Uptown Boogie Down Bronx New York Finest
