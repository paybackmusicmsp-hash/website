Booking Policy

How pricing and approval work

Pricing starts at the listed ranges and may change based on scope, turnaround, complexity, travel, and revisions. This launch version sends you to a Stripe Checkout deposit immediately after the booking is saved.

After Submission

What happens next

Your request is saved under your signed-in account.
You are redirected to secure Stripe Checkout.
Payment status syncs back into your booking record.
The team reviews scope, availability, and fit from there.

FAQ

Before You Submit

Can I book without an account?

No. Requests are attached to the signed-in client account so booking history and status stay tied to the correct person.

Is the listed price final?

No. The ranges are starting points. Final pricing is confirmed after review.

Can I send references?

Yes. This version stores your selected file names with the request so the team knows what references you planned to send.

Can weekends be booked?

The default booking calendar only allows weekday requests right now.
