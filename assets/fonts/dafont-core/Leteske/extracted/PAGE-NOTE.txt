Source: https://www.dafont.com/leteske.font

Leteske is a blackletter-inspired mono-width display font.
Use it however you like. Commercial use is fine.
If you find anything wrong with Leteske, let the author know via the email listed on the DaFont page.
The author also notes that Leteske was partially developed to be a typesetting font and recommends around 20pt for paragraphs, give or take.
